Stat 361 Philosophy

Statistics is a contact sport. We believe in learning through doing—using real-world datasets to test theoretical models. By balancing deep calculus proofs with hands-on exploration in R and JMP, we transform students from passive consumers of data into active investigators who can find truth in the noise of a messy world.

"Statistical thinking will one day be as necessary for effective citizenship as the ability to read and write." — H.G. Wells
