Pedagogical Framework

General Course Structure

1 Flipped Active Learning Environment

This class operates under a flipped classroom style. Rather than spending class time on passive lectures copying down definitions that you could read on your own, our time together is spent working through questions from the reading, discussion groups, and active in-class worksheets.

The Flipped Trade-Off: It requires more reading and class prep on your end before Monday, but it means less traditional homework because you get your core practice and problem solving done right in the room with instructor guidance and peer support.

2 Canvas Modules, Concept Checklists & Friday Labs

Every week has its own self-contained module in Canvas containing the assigned Spiegelhalter reading, supplementary OER text excerpts to clarify details, short video overviews, and a Concept Checklist of skills to master by week's end.

Friday Computer Lab (FMH B155): The instructor introduces the workflow for the first 10–15 minutes, then sets you free to work on your guided JMP software assignment. All lab assignments and related data files are posted directly in the weekly Canvas module.

✓ Weekly Readiness Check-in Quizzes (7% of Grade)

To ensure our flipped format discussions and worksheets run smoothly, a brief readiness check-in quiz is due in Canvas before Monday’s class. These focus strictly on the big-picture concepts from the reading—not technical trivia or arithmetic derivations.

The final question will always ask for your 'muddiest point' so we can tackle confusing topics together during class. Your lowest check-in quiz score is automatically dropped. Late readiness check-in quizzes are not accepted for credit.
